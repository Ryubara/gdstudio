# Pistol shot

- Source: GDStudio's own work, synthesised by code, nothing sampled and nothing third-party. They are the five
  variants of the House Pistol's shot from Deadnight (`game/assets/audio/guns/shot_house_pistol_1..5.ogg`), made by
  Deadnight's `tools/audio/gun_recipes_2.py` (a seeded report of a click, a crack, a roar, a bark and a low thump),
  copied here unchanged under the names below.
- Licence: MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 GDStudio contributors, the licence of the kit and of Deadnight (the files are
  covered by the repository's `LICENSE`, as Deadnight's own `docs/design/audio.md` records for its synthesised
  folders: no `LICENSE.md` there because nothing in them is third-party). Unlike the coin, the steps and the wind,
  these are not ElevenLabs output: no attribution is required and nothing here needs replacing before a commercial
  release.
