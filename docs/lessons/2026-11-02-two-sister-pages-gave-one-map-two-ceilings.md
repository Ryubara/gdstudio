# Two research pages written side by side gave one map two ceilings and two door widths

2026-11-02

- **Saw:** building Deadnight's second map (#631) to its two merged pages, the formula page asked a 3.0 to 3.6 m
  ceiling, a 3.3 to 4.0 m rise and 2.0 m doors (rows 9, 19, 27), while the art page's kit, already being modelled,
  had a 2.8 m ceiling, a 3.0 m storey and 1.4 m doors and stairs.
- **Cause:** the two pages were researched at once by two agents from the same images, each measuring the same
  heights its own way (a wall top against an eye height), and nobody compared their shared numbers before the kit and
  the greybox were started from them.
- **Fix:** the formula's rows are the hard targets, so the greybox follows them (a 3.4 m storey, 2.0 m doors and
  stairs) and the design page, the style bible's kit table and both research pages say so; the kit's 2.8 m modules
  take a 0.4 m band.
- **Check for next time:** when two pages feed one build, list the numbers both state (heights, widths, counts) side
  by side before any modelling starts, and settle each difference on the page that owns it.
- **Docs:** [Modular kits](../blender/modular-kits.md#planning-the-kit); Deadnight `docs/design/smallmap.md`.
