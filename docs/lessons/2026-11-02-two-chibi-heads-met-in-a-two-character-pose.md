# Two chibi heads met in a two-character pose

2026-11-02

- **Saw:** a reviver clip that looked right alone put the reviver's face in the downed teammate's in the game.
- **Cause:** the kit characters' heads are about 40 % of their height; a body leaning over its hands to a chest
  brings its head to the other head. One character in Blender cannot show it.
- **Fix:** keep the actor upright with the arms reaching out and down, aim at the belly, and check every
  two-character clip with both characters in the game capture.
- **Docs:** Deadnight `docs/research/character-animation.md` (Chibi heads collide).
