# New gun stocks came out as wedges

2026-11-03

- **Saw:** on the first turntable the PPSh's, MP5's and M4A1's stocks were tall wedges larger than their receivers
  (the PPSh's butt 0.20 m deep in drawing units, the MP5's and the M4A1's 0.16) (Deadnight #795).
- **Cause:** each stock was drawn from the real profile and then made chunky in every direction, as the receivers are;
  the kit keeps the receiver big and the stock shallow, a rule already on the weapons page that the first pass missed.
- **Fix:** butts 0.14-0.16 m deep and the MP5's and M4A1's stocks narrower (0.054-0.056 m across), the top line kept and
  the bottom line raised. Render a turntable beside a kit-style gun after the first build, before anything is solved
  against it.
- **Docs:** [Weapons, building by script](../tools/blender/weapons.md#building-by-script).
